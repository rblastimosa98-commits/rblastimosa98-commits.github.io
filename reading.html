Reading Books

Reading is one of the hobbies that has shaped the way I think and view the world. I especially enjoy books about human nature, psychology, leadership, discipline, and strategic thinking. Some of my favorite books include The 48 Laws of Power by Robert Greene and The Art of War by Sun Tzu.

These books help me understand different personalities, decision-making, and the importance of self-control and discipline. They have taught me how people think, how leaders make difficult decisions, and how strategy can be applied not only in conflict but also in everyday life.

Reading continuously expands my knowledge and encourages me to become a more disciplined, thoughtful, and prepared individual. It motivates me to improve myself every day and supports my goal of becoming a responsible public servant who can make wise decisions under pressure.
