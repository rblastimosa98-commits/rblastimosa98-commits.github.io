Running

Running is one of my favorite hobbies because it challenges both my body and my mind. Every run helps me become stronger, more disciplined, and mentally focused. It improves my endurance, keeps me physically fit, and reminds me that progress comes from consistency and determination.

Whether I am training for fitness or simply enjoying the outdoors, running gives me a sense of freedom and accomplishment. It teaches me to push beyond my limits, stay committed to my goals, and never give up even when the path becomes difficult.

As someone who dreams of serving in the Bureau of Fire Protection, running also prepares me for the physical demands of the profession. It strengthens my body, builds resilience, and develops the perseverance needed to serve others during emergencies.
