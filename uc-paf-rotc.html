About the Training

The University of the Cordilleras Philippine Air Force ROTC Training strengthened my discipline, leadership, teamwork, and sense of responsibility. Through military drills, leadership activities, and emergency preparedness exercises, I developed the confidence and resilience needed to serve my community and my country.

  • Leadership Development

  • Emergency Preparedness
  • Disaster Response Awareness
  • Physical Fitness
  • Patriotism and Service
